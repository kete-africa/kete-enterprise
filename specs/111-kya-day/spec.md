## Requirements

- **FR-001 — Where it comes from, in words.** « Pour moi » and « À faire » never show the key a feature
  files a task under; Kete's own tasks are tagged « Kete ».
- **FR-002 — A decision in words.** Its measure says what it is (« Risque · moyen »); a reference that
  is only Kete's own id is not shown.
- **FR-003 — Members as people know them.** A member known by her account only is one of the team; a
  client on several channels is one row, its channels said.
- **FR-004 — What Kete read, drawn.** The tables its tools read are kept with its turn and drawn under
  its answer.
- With kete-core: Kete's words carry no « agent » tag; a weekend day holding something is readable.
